ABSTRACT

Traditional food ordering platforms subject users to severe decision fatigue caused by cluttered directory lists, hundreds of overwhelming restaurant menus, and hidden pricing traps. Users struggle to answer three fundamental everyday questions: "What matches my current mood?", "What nutritious combinations can I get within my exact budget?", and "Can AI plan my macro-balanced meal instantly?".

To resolve these operational hurdles, CRAVO was engineered during a 6-week industrial training program at SHUBDEEP LABS. The application is an end-to-end modern web platform built using Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lenis Inertial Scrolling Engine.

Key Capabilities Engineered in the Platform:

  • Food Mood Sensory Matching Engine: Employs 9 psychological mood archetypes (Craving, Spicy Hit, Comfort, Healthy, Protein, Sweet Tooth, Cheat Day, Light, Party) with smooth auto-scroll to matched dishes.
  • Meals Under ₹100 Knapsack Optimizer: A dynamic programming combinatorial meal bundler that maximizes meal satisfaction (Main + Drink + Side) strictly under ₹100, ₹75, or ₹50 budgets.
  • Gamified ₹100 Challenge Arena: Interactive live budget liquid meter with real-time loyalty point rewards (+20 CRAVO Points) and celebration confetti upon building an optimized meal.
  • AI Meal Planner & Macro Tracker: Natural language chat interface with dynamic macro nutrient calculation (Protein, Carbs, Fats, Calories) and single-click cart injection.
  • Ultra-Smooth Inertial Scroll & Physics: 120fps Lenis physics engine, spring quantity counters, floating emoji particles, and seamless layout transitions.
📌 Industrial Training Outcome:

The project delivers a production-grade, highly responsive web application deployed with 100% test pass rates across functional test suites, meeting all MSBTE industrial training curriculum standards.

CHAPTER 2: INTRODUCTION & PROJECT OVERVIEW

2.1 Background of Food Tech & Decision Fatigue

Online food delivery has grown exponentially, yet modern consumer apps suffer from choice overload. When users open conventional platforms, they are inundated with hundreds of restaurant names, multi-page menus, and advertising banners. Studies show that users spend over 12 minutes simply deciding what to eat, frequently abandoning their carts due to frustration.

2.2 Problem Statement & Current Industry Challenges

  • Endless Directory Fatigue: Users are forced to filter by restaurant brand rather than by what they actually desire to eat.
  • Budget Unpredictability: Students and budget-conscious professionals cannot easily build complete multi-item meals (Main + Drink + Side) without exceeding budget limits after taxes and delivery fees.
  • Lack of Mood & Sensory Alignment: Food choices are heavily driven by emotional states (comfort, spicy thrill, clean diet, late-night party), which conventional platforms fail to capture.
  • Absence of Automated Macro Meal Planning: Fitness-oriented individuals lack instant AI tools to generate calorie and protein balanced meals in seconds.

2.3 Objectives & Vision of CRAVO Platform

CRAVO replaces the outdated directory model with the Anti-Directory Formula:

  1. Food Mood Engine: 9 curated emotional archetypes delivering targeted dish recommendations with smooth auto-scroll navigation.
  2. Meals Under ₹100 Solver: Algorithmic meal bundles (Main + Drink + Side) guaranteed under ₹100, ₹75, or ₹50.
  3. AI Food Planner: Instant macro-nutrient meal builder and conversational ordering assistant.

CHAPTER 4: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DATA DESIGN

4.1 Next.js App Router Architecture

CRAVO is structured around Next.js 15's unified App Router architecture. The architecture separates presentation components, context providers, and algorithmic solvers for maximum maintainability:

  • App Layer (app/): Modular routes (/food-mood, /under-100, /ai-planner, /explore, /orders, /profile, /admin).
  • Component Hierarchy (components/): Focused reusable modules (FoodCard, CartDrawer, Navbar, MobileNav, SmoothScrollProvider).
  • State Management (context/): Global AppContext handling cart persistence, user points, active filters, and toast notifications.
  • Algorithm Library (lib/): Knapsack solver (optimizer.ts) and macro calculations.

CHAPTER 5: CORE ALGORITHMS & HEURISTICS

5.1 Knapsack Dynamic Programming Budget Optimizer

To solve the multi-item budget combination problem, CRAVO implements a bounded 0/1 Knapsack Dynamic Programming Optimizer tailored for food bundles. Given a budget constraint $W \in \{50, 75, 100, 150\}$, items with price $p_i$ and sensory weight $v_i$:

Maximize ∑ vi × xi   subject to  ∑ pi × xi ≤ Budget
where xi ∈ {0, 1} and Category_Diversity ≥ 2 (Main + Beverage/Side)

The algorithm guarantees maximum culinary variety and nutritional balance without exceeding the user's selected budget.

5.2 Sensory Food Mood Matching Heuristic

Each dish is tagged with multi-dimensional sensory scores across 9 mood vectors: $$\text{MatchScore}(Dish, Mood) = w_1 \cdot \text{SpiceLevel} + w_2 \cdot \text{Protein} + w_3 \cdot \text{ComfortIndex} + w_4 \cdot \text{Rating}$$ When a mood card is clicked, dishes are sorted in descending order of MatchScore and scrolled seamlessly into view.
